**[Sossoldi](https://github.com/RIP-Comm/sossoldi)** is a free and open source wealth management app: it tracks accounts, investments, income, expenses and budgets, and turns them into one number — your net worth. It is written in Flutter and runs on iOS and Android.

## What it does today

- **Net worth**: assets minus liabilities, recalculated on every change.
- **Accounts**: bank accounts, cash, investments and property, in multiple currencies.
- **Transactions**: income, expenses and transfers with categories and subcategories, searchable.
- **Budgets**: a monthly limit per category, with the remaining amount visible.
- **Recurring payments**: scheduled once and counted when they fall due.
- **Charts**: spending by month and by category.
- **Backup**: full CSV export and import. Data stays local, there is no Sossoldi account or server.

The current release is **1.0.0**, published on 20 August 2026 and available from the App Store, Google Play and F-Droid.

## What is not there yet

- **Open banking** is in the codebase but not reachable from the interface: no transactions are imported from a bank automatically.
- **Cloud sync** does not exist. Moving data between devices means exporting and importing a CSV file.
- **Translations**: the released app is in English; Italian and Portuguese are in progress.
